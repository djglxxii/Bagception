What lies within

It looks like a bag. It is not a bag.

Undo the clasp and look inside. There is no bottom. Past the leather, where the lining ought to be, a small grey sky folds away into the distance. This is the Hollow: a pocket universe no larger than a cottage garden, stitched shut by a wizard nobody remembers, and forgotten ever since in the lining of an ordinary-looking sack.

Nineteen smaller pockets hang in that sky like moons. Each is a world of its own, and each keeps one kind of thing. One is cold and dim and full of the sound of glass: potions go there. One smells of paper and candle smoke: books and letters drift down into it like autumn leaves. One is small, bright and a little greedy, and it chimes when coins arrive. The last and largest is a cheerful fog where everything nobody else wanted ends up, and no one looks at it too closely.

Drop anything into Bagception and the Hollow decides where it belongs. The item slips out of your hand, into the grey, and settles into its pocket without a sound. The sorting is done by something. Scholars disagree about what, and Bagception does not answer questions.

And because what goes in has left Faerûn entirely, Faerûn forgets how much it weighs. A greataxe inside the Hollow weighs exactly as much as a greataxe on the far side of the moon: nothing at all. Take it out, and the world remembers at once.

Gale has a theory about all this. It takes forty minutes to explain and involves a diagram. We recommend nodding.

The serious part

Baldur's Gate 3 already gives you a handful of specialised containers: the Alchemy Pouch, the Camp Supply Sack, the Keychain, and whatever pouches and satchels you loot along the way. Each one solves a small piece of the inventory problem, and together they become a new one: a row of little bags you have to open, remember and manage on their own.

Bagception was made to be the one bag that replaces all of them. Every kind of item has a home inside it, and the vanilla containers' jobs are done by bags of its own:

  • the Reagent Pouch does the Alchemy Pouch's work,
  • the Larder Pack does the Camp Supply Sack's,
  • the Key Ring does the Keychain's,

and sixteen more cover everything else, from weapons and armour to dyes, coatings and gold. Once Bagception is on your back, the vanilla containers have nothing left to do, and you are free to sell them, stash them at camp or leave them in a ditch outside Waukeen's Rest. In fact, it works best when you do: a vanilla pouch you still carry may grab an item before Bagception can.

Everything inside weighs nothing. Every item in Bagception or one of its bags counts for zero against your carrying capacity, however heavy it is. Stow full plate, a greataxe and forty-one wheels of cheese, and your carried weight does not move. Take an item out and its weight returns. Anything already in Bagception when you update becomes weightless the next time you load the save.

Every party member gets one. Bagception is given to each companion when they join the party, and to everyone already in your party when you add the mod to a save in progress.

Alchemy ingredients, camp supplies, keys and gold you pick up in the world go straight into their bags without being touched. Everything else is sorted the moment you drop it onto Bagception.

Good to know

  • Bagception is loyal. It and its bags cannot be dropped, sent to camp, given away or put in a chest, so nothing can get lost. The same loyalty hides it from traders. To sell something, drag it out of Bagception into your own inventory first.
  • The number on Bagception counts its own bags, so an empty Bagception shows 19. The pockets insist on being counted, and the game agrees with them.
  • A bag added mid-session may look empty until you reload the save once. It works normally in the meantime.
  • Only what you put into Bagception is sorted. Items already in your inventory stay where you left them. Bags and pouches from the base game or other mods also stay at the top level, unsorted, so they are never opened up or rearranged.
  • Other containers you put inside weigh nothing themselves, but their own contents keep their weight. Empty them into Bagception instead.
  • Updates reach your save. If a later version adds a bag, your existing Bagceptions receive it when the save loads.
  • Uninstalling mid-playthrough is at your own risk, as the game warns: empty Bagception first. Items that were inside may stay weightless.

Requirements

None. Bagception uses Larian's own mod system and nothing else: no Script Extender and no other mods required.
