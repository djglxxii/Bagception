## Full description

### What lies within

Bagception is less a bag and more a doorway.

Open it, and somewhere beyond the lining is a tiny pocket universe where gravity never got around to showing up. Swords, armour, books, barrels, wheels of cheese. Everything simply hangs there, politely refusing to weigh anything at all.

The smaller bags inside are little gateways of their own, each leading to a different corner of this impossible place. Drop something into Bagception and whatever magic runs the place quietly sends it where it belongs.

Take an item back out, and Faerûn remembers gravity exists.

Gale has theories. Naturally.

### The serious part

Baldur's Gate 3 already gives you a handful of specialised containers: the Alchemy Pouch,
the Camp Supply Sack, the Keychain, and whatever pouches and satchels you loot along the
way. Each one solves a small piece of the inventory problem, and together they become a
new one: a row of little bags you have to open, remember and manage on their own.

Bagception was made to be the **one bag that replaces all of them**. Every kind of item
has a home inside it, and the vanilla containers' jobs are done by bags of its own:

- the **Reagent Pouch** does the Alchemy Pouch's work,
- the **Larder Pack** does the Camp Supply Sack's,
- the **Key Ring** does the Keychain's,

and sixteen more cover everything else, from weapons and armour to dyes, coatings and gold.
Once Bagception is on your back, the vanilla containers have nothing left to do, and you are
free to sell them, stash them at camp or leave them in a ditch outside Waukeen's Rest. In
fact, it works best when you do: a vanilla pouch you still carry may grab an item before
Bagception can.

**Everything inside weighs nothing.** Every item in Bagception or one of its bags counts
for zero against your carrying capacity, however heavy it is. Stow full plate, a greataxe
and forty-one wheels of cheese, and your carried weight does not move. Take an item out
and its weight returns. Anything already in Bagception when you update becomes weightless
the next time you load the save.

**Every party member gets one.** Bagception is given to each companion when they join the
party, and to everyone already in your party when you add the mod to a save in progress.

### The nineteen bags

| Bag | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Weapon Roll | Weapons |
| Shield Rack | Shields |
| Armour Trunk | Armour, clothing, helmets, boots, gloves and cloaks |
| Jewelry Box | Rings and amulets |
| Quiver | Arrows |
| Scroll Case | Scrolls |
| Potion Case | Potions |
| Elixir Rack | Elixirs |
| Coating Kit | Weapon coatings, oils and poisons |
| Grenade Satchel | Grenades, bombs and anything else meant to be thrown |
| Reagent Pouch | Alchemy ingredients and extracts. *Collects on pickup.* |
| Larder Pack | Food, drink and camp supplies. *Collects on pickup.* |
| Book Satchel | Books, letters, notes, maps and journals |
| Key Ring | Keys. *Collects on pickup.* |
| Tool Roll | Thieves' tools, kits, shovels, instruments, torches and lanterns |
| Dye Pouch | Dyes |
| Quest Satchel | Quest items, checked before every other bag. Keys stay on the Key Ring. |
| Coin Purse | Gold. *Collects on pickup.* Gold in the purse still counts at traders. |
| Odds Sack | Everything else: gems, silverware, trinkets, and whatever that is |

Alchemy ingredients, camp supplies, keys and gold you pick up in the world go straight into
their bags without being touched. Everything else is sorted the moment you drop it onto
Bagception.

### Good to know

- **Bagception is loyal.** It and its bags cannot be dropped, sent to camp, given away or
  put in a chest, so nothing can get lost. The same loyalty hides it from traders. **To
  sell something, drag it out of Bagception into your own inventory first.**
- **The number on Bagception counts its own bags**, so an empty Bagception shows 19. The
  pockets insist on being counted, and the game agrees with them.
- **A bag added mid-session may look empty until you reload the save once.** It works
  normally in the meantime.
- **Only what you put into Bagception is sorted.** Items already in your inventory stay where
  you left them. Bags and pouches from the base game or other mods also stay at the top
  level, unsorted, so they are never opened up or rearranged.
- **Other containers you put inside** weigh nothing themselves, but their own contents
  keep their weight. Empty them into Bagception instead.
- **Updates reach your save.** If a later version adds a bag, your existing Bagceptions
  receive it when the save loads.
- **Uninstalling mid-playthrough** is at your own risk, as the game warns: empty
  Bagception first. Items that were inside may stay weightless.

### Requirements

None. Bagception uses Larian's own mod system and nothing else: **no Script Extender and no
other mods required.**
